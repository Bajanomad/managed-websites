About Your Agent

Hola, I am Marisol Vega.

I was born in La Paz and have lived in El Pescadero for the last fourteen years. I know which streets flood in September, which wells run sweet, and which sunsets you will be watching from your terrace before you sign anything.

Buying in Baja involves fideicomisos, ejido history and paperwork that looks nothing like a closing back home. My job is to walk you through all of it in plain language, in English or Spanish, and to tell you when a property is not right for you, even when it costs me the sale.

No pressure, no rush. Just local knowledge and straight answers.
