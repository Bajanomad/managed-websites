Her Story

Cyndi came to Cabo the way her clients do: on a visit, in a small condo at Marina Sol, and she simply never left. That was 2007. She had already spent 25 years as a broker in California. She has owned a hotel, a restaurant, and a piece of this coast's real estate history, and she ran one of Pescadero's beloved bars, which is one reason everybody on this coastline knows her name.

These days she keeps a short client list on purpose. Fewer people, looked after properly. If you are one of her people, she answers. If you want to become one of her people, start with a conversation.

"Don't dream it, do it. Call me to make it happen."
