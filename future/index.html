Ten Sites From 2031

Rough wireframes for Cyndi Williams. No photos, on purpose: the tiles say what goes in them. Palette: black, white, cream, gold. Bones borrowed from Jade Mills and Aaron Kirman, and from the Kenny build, then pushed five years forward.
2021: brochure sites. Static galleries, hand-typed listings, search bolted on, every agent site the same template underneath. TODAY: video heroes, live MLS feeds, letter-roll-up names, chat bubbles, the first personalization. The template mills sell exactly this, which is why it is already worthless as a distinction. 2031: the ten directions below. Sites that act, remember, prove, and serve two audiences at once, wrapped in the restraint the template crowd cannot copy because copying it requires taste.
