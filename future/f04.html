The Signature Entrance

Motion is the logo. She introduces herself.
2021: static logo, stock hero photo. Today: Jade's letter roll-up over a video montage. 2031: the entrance is a person, not a picture; the name animation hands off to her recorded self.
Frame 1 · The name
CYNDI WILLIAMS, letter by letter, rolling up from a mask, grey to white, gold on the final letter.
Jade's move, evolved: 2 seconds, over live Cerritos surf at this hour, not a stock loop.
Frame 2 · The greeting
Her face, recorded: "Hi, I am Cyndi. Let me show you my coast." 8 seconds, skippable, remembered.
The presenter replaces the hero image. She is the interface.
Frame 3 · The handoff
Three doors only: BUY THE COAST · SELL WITH ME · JUST TALK
Kirman's restraint, Jade's serif, her voice. Everything else waits behind the menu, which rolls up in the same letter animation.
