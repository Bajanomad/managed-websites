The Quiet Page

Radical restraint as the anti-template weapon.
2021: more features won. Today: Jade and Kirman win by showing less. 2031: when every site can generate everything, the luxury is a site that chooses.
Screen one
One photograph. One line: "Cerritos Beachfront Acre." One price. Nothing else.
In a web built from the same 10 templates, silence is the loudest luxury signal left.
Screen two
One sentence of story. The surf, the light, the lot at 6 pm.
Scroll is chapters, not sections. Each screen earns the next.
Screen three
One button: "Walk it with Cyndi."
No forms, no popups, no chat bubble begging. One door, opened by a human.
Every other property
Exists only when asked for, by voice or by the menu.
The full book of 34 is one question away. Restraint up front, depth on demand.
