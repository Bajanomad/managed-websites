Audience of One

One address, a different site for every visitor.
2021: one page for everyone. Today: A/B tests and cookies. 2031: the page assembles itself around declared intent in a second or two, and says so.
Same URL, buyer version
Hero: Cerritos at surf height. First tiles: lots, financing, "walk to the break".
The page read his search, his saves, his country. It opens where he left off.
Same URL, seller version
Hero: her sold wall. First tiles: "what your street did this quarter", valuation.
A neighbor thinking of selling never sees the buyer show. One address, two sites.
The assembly seam
A quiet line at the foot: "This page was arranged for you. See the standard version."
Personalization with the curtain open. Luxury does not hide its machinery.
Memory tile
"Welcome back. Still watching Las Tunas?"
Returning visitors are greeted like regulars at her old bar. Recognition is the brand.
Anonymous tile
No data? The classic Jade-style page, untouched.
Personalization is a layer, never a ransom. The elegant default always exists.
