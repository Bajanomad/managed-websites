The Concierge

The website is her staff, not her brochure.
2021: pages you browse. Today: a chat bubble in the corner. 2031: the conversation IS the site; pages are generated per visitor, per question.
First thing on screen
"Good morning. Three of your listings changed this week. Shall I walk you through them?"
No homepage. No menu. The site opens as a conversation with her agent, who already knows why you came. Jade's letter roll-up becomes her name signing itself above the greeting.
Dialogue tile
The visitor talks or types. The page builds itself around the answer.
Ask for beachfront under a million and the listings assemble below the conversation, live from MLS.
Action tile
Buttons that DO things: "Book a showing Saturday", "Send me the comps".
Not links. Executed tasks, confirmed on screen, synced to her calendar.
Trust tile
"I am Cyndi's assistant. She reviews everything I send."
The agent states its limits up front. Class is honesty about what is human and what is not.
