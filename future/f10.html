Built For Two Visitors

Half the audience of 2031 is not human.
2021: sites built for people, scraped by bots. Today: AI answers questions about her listings, often from stale data. 2031: the site serves people an experience and their agents the facts, from one source.
The human surface
Everything the other nine concepts show a person.
Beauty, story, restraint. Built for eyes and feelings.
The agent surface
The same truth, structured: every listing, price, status, and term, machine-readable, always current.
By 2031 half her visitors arrive as software: a buyer's own AI, sent to scout. It needs facts, not adjectives.
The introduction
"Your assistant asked about Beach Lot 7. Here is what it found, and what it could not know: the light at 6 pm."
Her site and the visitor's agent talk first. Then she adds the one thing machines cannot scout: judgment.
The standard
One source of truth under both surfaces, so the story and the data can never disagree.
The anti-template moat: anyone can copy the look. Nobody can copy her book, her voice, and her data agreeing with each other.
