Our Story

A family business since 1987

Casa Textil began with one wooden loom and our grandfather, Don Refugio Aguilar, who walked his blankets from ranch to ranch before opening a single room shop in Todos Santos. Today his grandchildren run the store, and the same loom still stands by the front window.

Each blanket starts as raw highland wool. We card it, spin it, and dye it with cochineal, indigo, and marigold, the way it has been done in Mexico for centuries. Then the loom takes over: warp, weft, and three days of steady hands before the fringe is knotted one strand at a time.

The silver comes from partner families in Taxco we have worked with for over thirty years, and every textile on our shelves was made by a person whose name we know. That is the whole business, really. People we know, making things that last.
